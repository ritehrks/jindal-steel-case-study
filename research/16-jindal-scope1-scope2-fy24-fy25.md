# Jindal Stainless — Scope 1 + Scope 2 Emissions Analysis
FY2024 vs FY2025 | BRSR / Integrated Reporting Data

## Executive summary
Jindal Stainless disclosed Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse-gas emissions together with Scope 1+2 emission intensity in its FY2024-25 sustainability disclosures. The reported Scope 1+2 emission intensity decreased from 2.15 tCO₂e per tonne of crude steel (tCO₂e/tcs) in FY24 to 1.85 tCO₂e/tcs in FY25.

On the absolute-emissions basis, Scope 1+2 emissions decreased by 161,165 tCO₂e, or 4.26%, from FY24 to FY25. On the intensity basis, the reduction was 0.30 tCO₂e/tcs, equivalent to 13.95%.

## 1. Reported emissions and intensity
| Metric | FY24 | FY25 | Absolute change | YoY change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scope 1 | 2,992,334 tCO₂e | 2,995,799 tCO₂e | +3,465 tCO₂e | +0.12% |
| Scope 2 — market based | 787,141 tCO₂e | 622,511 tCO₂e | −164,630 tCO₂e | −20.92% |
| **Scope 1 + Scope 2** | **3,779,475 tCO₂e** | **3,618,310 tCO₂e** | **−161,165 tCO₂e** | **−4.26%** |
| **Scope 1 + 2 intensity** | **2.15 tCO₂e/tcs** | **1.85 tCO₂e/tcs** | **−0.30 tCO₂e/tcs** | **−13.95%** |

## 2. Absolute Scope 1 + Scope 2 calculation
- **FY24 total Scope 1 + Scope 2:** 2,992,334 + 787,141 = 3,779,475 tCO₂e
- **FY25 total Scope 1 + Scope 2:** 2,995,799 + 622,511 = 3,618,310 tCO₂e
- **Absolute reduction:** 3,779,475 − 3,618,310 = 161,165 tCO₂e
- **Percentage reduction:** (161,165 ÷ 3,779,475) × 100 = 4.264% ≈ 4.26%

**Interpretation:** Absolute Scope 1+2 emissions fell by approximately 4.26% year over year. Scope 1 increased marginally, while the larger decrease in market-based Scope 2 more than offset that increase.

## 3. Exact emission-intensity ratio and reduction
- **FY24 reported Scope 1+2 intensity:** 2.15 tCO₂e/tcs
- **FY25 reported Scope 1+2 intensity:** 1.85 tCO₂e/tcs
- **Intensity reduction:** 2.15 − 1.85 = 0.30 tCO₂e/tcs
- **Percentage reduction:** (0.30 ÷ 2.15) × 100 = 13.95%

**Key reported ratio:** FY25 Scope 1+2 emission intensity = 1.85 tCO₂e per tonne of crude steel (tCO₂e/tcs).

## 4. Absolute reduction vs intensity reduction
| Basis | Reduction | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Absolute Scope 1+2 emissions | 161,165 tCO₂e (4.26%) | Change in total reported emissions |
| Scope 1+2 emission intensity | 0.30 tCO₂e/tcs (13.95%) | Change in emissions per tonne of crude steel |

These two percentages should not be treated as interchangeable. Intensity reflects emissions relative to steel production, so it can fall faster than absolute emissions when production/output increases.

## 5. Target context
Jindal Stainless has stated a target of reducing Scope 1+2 emission intensity by 50% by FY2035 from its FY2022 baseline. The FY2022 baseline cited in the company's climate disclosure is 1.98 tCO₂e/tcs.
