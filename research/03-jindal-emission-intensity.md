# Jindal Stainless — Disclosed Emission Intensity (FY22–FY26)

Source: Gemini Deep Research synthesis from JSL ESG Factsheets, BRSR, CDP Climate
Change Reports, and Integrated Annual Reports.

## Combined Scope 1 + Scope 2 Intensity

| Fiscal Year | GHG Intensity (Scope 1+2) | Reporting Basis | Source Document |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY22 (Baseline) | **1.98 tCO2e/tcs** | Consolidated Company-Wide | ESG Factsheet FY24-25, p.3/4; ESG Factsheet FY26 |
| FY23 | **2.08 tCO2e/tcs** | Consolidated Company-Wide | ESG Factsheet FY24-25, p.3/4; ESG Factsheet FY26 |
| FY24 (Peak) | **2.15 tCO2e/tcs** | Consolidated Company-Wide | ESG Factsheet FY24-25; CDP FY23-24 |
| FY25 | **1.85 tCO2e/tcs** | Consolidated Company-Wide | ESG Factsheet FY24-25; CDP FY25 |
| FY26 | **1.76 tCO2e/tcs** | Consolidated Company-Wide | ESG Factsheet FY26 |

### Trajectory
- FY22→FY24: Intensity ROSE from 1.98 to 2.15 (+8.5%) due to hyper-scale capacity expansion
- FY24→FY26: Intensity DROPPED from 2.15 to 1.76 (-18.1%) as decarbonization investments activated
- FY22→FY26: Net reduction of **11.1%** from baseline
- FY35 target: 50% reduction from FY22 baseline = **0.99 tCO2e/tcs**

- 91 energy conservation projects at Hisar over 3 years
- Hisar SEC: 8.84 GJ/ton (outperforms international benchmarks)
- PAT Cycle-2: 6.3% reduction in specific power consumption
- Green H2 (Hisar): 90 Nm³/hr, abates ~2,700 tCO2e/yr
- Captive solar (Jajpur): 44.3 million kWh/yr, offsets 32,208 tCO2e/yr
- Planned: 300 MW hybrid renewable (wind+solar) for Jajpur
- Waste heat recovery: 117,299 metric tonnes in FY25
- Internal Carbon Pricing (ICP) framework in advanced deployment
- Digital sustainability platform integrated with SAP S/4HANA ERP
